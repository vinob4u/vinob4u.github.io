Yellow: anatomical, soiled (dressings, plaster casts), expired/discarded medicines, chemical, microbiology and lab waste. Red: contaminated recyclable plastics (tubing, IV sets, catheters, urine bags, syringes without needles, gloves). White: sharps (needles, syringes with fixed needles, scalpels, blades) in puncture-proof translucent containers. Blue: glassware and metallic body implants.
