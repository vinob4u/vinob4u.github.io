Privacy Policy – RDA Duty Roster

Last updated: 7 October 2026

RDA Duty Roster (“the app”) is published by the Resident Doctors Association, AIIMS Mangalagiri. It shows the monthly duty roster of doctors at AIIMS Mangalagiri so that staff can quickly see who is on duty and contact them.

Information the app collects from you

None. The app has no sign-in, no forms that send data anywhere, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We do not collect, store or share any personal information about the people who use the app.

Information stored on your device

If a roster in-charge uses the “Update roster” feature, the pasted roster is kept only in that device's browser storage so it can be previewed. It is never sent to us or to anyone else, and it can be removed with “Undo local changes” or by clearing the app's data.

Information shown in the app

The app displays the duty roster issued by AIIMS Mangalagiri departments, limited to doctors' names, departments and duty assignments, for the purpose of hospital duty coordination. The app does not publish doctors' phone numbers. Doctors who want their details corrected can contact the Resident Doctors Association at the address below.

Children

The app is intended for hospital staff and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
